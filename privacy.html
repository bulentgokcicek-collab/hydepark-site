Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

At Hyde Park Speakers, accessible from hydeparkspeakers.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Hyde Park Speakers and how we use it.

Log Files

Hyde Park Speakers follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Hyde Park Speakers uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

Mobile Applications Privacy Policy

This section applies to the mobile applications developed under the Hyde Park Speakers brand, including: Place Them All, Just Swipe Away, Search Your Memory, and Lingo Match.

Data Collection and Usage

Our mobile applications do not collect or request any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location). Any game progress, settings, and high scores are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

To keep our games free, we integrate third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device. Specifically, our games use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). AdMob may collect and process telemetry data, such as your IP address, device model, advertising identifier (e.g., Google AD ID), and ad interaction logs. This data is used solely to deliver relevant advertisements and analyze ad performance. You can manage or reset your advertising identifier via your Android device settings.

Children's Privacy

Our games do not target children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take necessary actions.

Consent

By using our website and mobile applications, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
